# Worked examples — see the whole flow before you spend the effort

Two complete runs, end to end: the messy email that went in, and the ledger and
ranked list that came out. Nothing is trimmed to look good — the refusals are
in here too, because that is half of what you are buying.

| File | What it shows |
| --- | --- |
| `01-hvac-backlog-sweep.md` | A 6-truck HVAC contractor pastes a month of unanswered threads. Bulk classification, two refusals, one email split into two rows because it carried two jobs, an invoice kept OPEN because "the check is coming" is a promise not a payment, follow-up windows by kind of thread, and a ranked list that finds $18,400 sitting on a forgotten quote. |
| `02-bookkeeper-single-triage.md` | One thread, triaged. Shows the money facts being pulled, one email logged as two rows on two clocks, the "quote requested" window, and the draft gate stopping TWICE — once for a number it does not have, then again when the answer comes back as a range instead of a figure. |

**These are examples, not your data.** Nothing in here ever goes into your
ledger. When you are ready, open your own email, copy your unanswered threads,
and say **"sweep my backlog."** The flow you see below is exactly what runs on
yours — same gates, same windows, same ranking, your customers and your
numbers.

The people and companies in these examples are made up for the demonstration.
The shapes are not: they are the exchanges that actually clog a small
contractor's inbox.
