office manager, quotes go out from his phone between jobs.

not answered, and pasted the pile in one go with the words "sweep my backlog."

## What Dave got out of four minutes

He knew about Karen. He had forgotten Tony entirely — an $18,400 quote, 13 days
silent, one day from cold. That single line is worth more than the kit costs,
and it was sitting in his inbox the whole time.

He also got two honest gaps instead of two invented rows, which is why he trusts
the other five.

**Notice what did NOT happen:** nothing was sent. Every draft comes back to Dave
to read and send from his own inbox. The kit sorts, ranks, and writes; the
person still decides and still hits send.
