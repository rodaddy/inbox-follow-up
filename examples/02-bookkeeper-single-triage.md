**Who:** Annette Vail, Vail Bookkeeping. Two-person shop in Patchogue, ~30
small-business clients.

**What she did:** pasted one thread and said "triage this."

**Today, for this run:** 2026-08-11.

---


## What matters in this run

The kit did the job a bare chat would not have: it **stopped twice**. A generic
AI asked for a reply would have produced a polite email that said "I'll get you
a payroll quote shortly" and dodged the filing question, and Greg would have
walked into his partner meeting with nothing. Worse, an AI guessing at a
plausible payroll price would have put a wrong number in front of a partner
under Annette's name.

The second stop is the one people do not expect. Annette answered — and her
answer was a range. Most tools would have taken it, written "$300-400" or
quietly averaged it to $350, and Annette would have found out she had quoted
$300 the next time Greg brought it up. A range in an email is a promise of its
lowest number. The kit said so, offered the two real options, and made her
choose.

One extra question, ten seconds of Annette's time, and the reply actually
answers what was asked — with a number she can defend.

**Notice what did NOT happen:** nothing was sent. The kit wrote it; Annette
reads it, changes what she wants, and sends it from her own inbox.
